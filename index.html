Atlas Map Device Guide

Rules, Currency Tiers & Shortcuts

Core Objective

Click ACTIVATE (or click the central Astrolabe while idle) to open a 60s T16 Delirium Map or Endless run. Rapidly click high-tier FilterBlade currency labels before they despawn (1.4s–1.8s) to build your streak multiplier up to 5.0x.

Map Integrity (3 Ruby Gems)

Avoid clicking low-tier clutter (Scroll of Wisdom, Orb of Transmutation, Orb of Augmentation, Armourer's Scrap, Regal Shard). Each minus-value click breaks one Integrity gem and resets your streak. Clicking 3 clutter items immediately ends the map run.

Breach, Abyss, Vaal Altar & Controls

  • Central Astrolabe: Click while the Map Device is idle to activate your selected map mode.
  • Breach & Abyss Charge Gauges: Picking valuable currency charges the BREACH and ABYSS dock plates to 100%. Click them (or press 1/Q for Breach, 2/E for Abyss) to unleash a high-tier surge on demand.
  • Rare Vaal Altar (50/50): Click glowing crimson Vaal Altars for a 50/50 gamble — win to drop half of all valuable currencies you picked up back onto the ground, or brick to lose 1 Integrity gem.
  • Z or Dock Switch: Toggle FilterBlade ground loot label visibility (LABELS: ON / OFF).
  • SPACE: Pop an immediate Breach/Abyss pack during a run.
  • M: Mute / unmute tier drop audio (S–S++, A+, and A–B custom drop sounds).
