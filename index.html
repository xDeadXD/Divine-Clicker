Atlas Map Device Guide

Rules, Currency Tiers & Shortcuts

Core Objective

Click ACTIVATE (or click the central Astrolabe) to open a 60s T16 Delirium Map or Endless run. Rapidly click high-tier currency labels before they despawn (1.4s–1.8s) to build your streak multiplier up to 5.0x.

Integrity & Clutter Hazards

You begin with 3/3 Integrity crimson gems. Clicking minus-value clutter (Scroll of Wisdom, Orb of Transmutation, Orb of Augmentation, Armourer's Scrap, Regal Shard) deducts points, resets your streak to 1.0x, and shatters one Integrity gem. Losing all 3 gems ends the map immediately.

Breach, Abyss & Controls

  • Central Astrolabe: Click during a map to pulse the compass and burst bonus currency drops around the Atlas.
  • Breach & Abyss Encounters: Click glowing purple Breach or green Abyss icons on the map floor for high-tier loot explosions.
  • Z or Dock Switch: Toggle ground loot label visibility (LABELS: ON / OFF).
  • SPACE: Pop an immediate Breach/Abyss pack during a run.
  • M: Mute / unmute drop audio (S-Tier and Below S-Tier custom drop sounds).
